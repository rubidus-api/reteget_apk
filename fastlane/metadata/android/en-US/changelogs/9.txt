Latest: a preset can name an index page (a directory listing, or GitHub's releases API) and Latest downloads the newest file there that fits the preset URL, sorted as Windows does (0.3.10 after 0.3.9). The rete presets use it. Upgrades: when a download upgrades an installed app with the same signing key, ReteGet installs it without asking; Android 12+ may also skip its own screen.
