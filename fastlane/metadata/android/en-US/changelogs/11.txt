Broken HTTP(S) downloads continue where they stopped: automatically up to three times, and with Retry, also after the app was closed (needs a server that sends an ETag or Last-Modified; a file that changed is fetched again). Password-protected servers: put the user name and password in the URL (http://user:password@host/path); ReteGet answers with Digest (SHA-256, MD5) or Basic only when the server asks, and only to that address. Passwords are shown as *** and left out of settings exports.
